Alps to Tokyo Motor Club
=======================

Extract the entire archive, then open index.html in a current Chrome or Edge.
The game runs offline and uses WebGL 2. A first drive starts outside the garage in Chamonix.

Garage selects your vehicle.
Follow the Tokyo signs at the village junction and continue over the connector into Tokyo.
The dog walks along the pavement beside the garage.

Driving
WASD or arrows: steer, accelerate, brake. Hold S/Down from a stop to reverse.
Space: handbrake. E: exit at a stop or approach and enter another vehicle.
V: Chase, Hood, Cabin, and Wide. C: toggle rear-facing view.
On foot: WASD or arrows to walk, Shift to sprint, mouse drag to orbit.
Escape: pause or go back. Enter: skip the intro or confirm a showroom bay.

When you walk or drive out of the garage, unoccupied vehicles return to their bays.
The minimap shows nearby roads and traffic. It can change to a wider view with M.
Performance, Balanced and High change rendering resolution.

Editable source is in source/. To rebuild game.js from that directory:
npm install --no-save three@0.180.0 esbuild@0.25.11
npx esbuild main.js --bundle --minify --outfile=../game.js --format=iife --target=es2022 --legal-comments=eof

The model assets came from the supplied project files. Three.js license is included.
